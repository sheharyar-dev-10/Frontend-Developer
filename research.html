Research at the university

Faculty and students work on projects in health, chemistry, environment, social sciences and technology. Several institutes on campus are known nationally and internationally.

Research areas

Chemical and biological sciences

Drug discovery, natural products and molecular research.

Environment and marine science

Coastal ecosystems, climate and water.

Social sciences

Urban studies, economics, media and public policy.

Computing and data

Software, data analysis and applied computing.
