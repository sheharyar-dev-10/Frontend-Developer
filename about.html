WHO WE ARE

A Leading Public University

The University of Karachi was established in 1951 under an act of parliament. It began teaching and research in 1953 with the Faculties of Arts and Science.

In 1960, the university moved to its present campus on University Road, Karachi. Today, it serves as an important centre for higher education and research in Pakistan.

The university offers educational opportunities in science, engineering, arts, management, pharmacy, law and other disciplines.
