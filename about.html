About the university

History, mission and the people behind the campus.

Our history

The University of Karachi was established in 1951 to serve the growing city and the new country. It later moved to its present campus in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, where most departments are located today.

Over the decades it has grown into a large teaching and research university, with graduates working in government, industry, science, law, media and education across Pakistan and abroad.

Mission and vision

Mission

Provide quality higher education, support original research and prepare graduates who serve society.

Vision

Be a leading university in the region, known for academic freedom, merit and community service.

Values

Integrity, curiosity, inclusion and respect for knowledge.

Timeline

  1. 1951 The University of Karachi is established.
  2. 1960s Move to the new campus on University Road begins.
  3. Today Multiple faculties, institutes and research centres on one campus.
