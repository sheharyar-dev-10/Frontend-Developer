How to apply

  1. Read the admission announcement and check eligibility.
  2. Fill in the online application form.
  3. Pay the application fee and upload the required documents.
  4. Appear in the entry test or interview if required.
  5. Check the merit list and submit your dues on time.

Dates, fees and criteria change every session. Always confirm them on the official university notice.
