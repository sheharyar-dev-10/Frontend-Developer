Campus life

Library, societies, sports and a place to call your own.

Life on campus

The campus is large and green, with open lawns, canteens and study spaces between departments. Students spend as much time learning from each other as from lectures.

Facilities

Library

Reading halls, journals, theses and digital resources.

Hostels

On-campus accommodation for eligible students.

Sports

Grounds and courts for cricket, football and indoor games.

Transport

Point buses serve many parts of the city.

Societies and events

Student societies run debates, drama, music, tech competitions and welfare drives. Join one in your first semester and you will meet students from every department.
